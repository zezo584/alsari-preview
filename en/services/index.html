Inverter technology

Why choose an energy-saving inverter AC?

An inverter compressor runs at variable speeds instead of constantly switching on and off. It keeps a steady temperature and uses noticeably less electricity than conventional units, with quieter operation and a longer lifespan.

  • Lower power use and smaller bills
  • Steady temperature without swings
  • Quieter operation day and night
  • Cooling in summer, heating in winter

What is the difference between an inverter and a regular AC?

A regular AC repeatedly switches on and off at full power, while an inverter adjusts compressor speed as needed, keeping a steady temperature, using less electricity and running more quietly.

How do I know the right AC capacity for my room?

Capacity depends on room size, orientation, insulation and occupancy. Roughly, 1 ton suits small rooms, 1.5 ton medium rooms and 2 ton large living areas. Contact us for a free, accurate consultation.

Do you provide installation?

Yes. Our technicians install units to proper standards, including leak testing and a test run before handover.

How often does my AC need maintenance?

We recommend servicing twice a year, before summer and before winter, and cleaning the filters every two to four weeks during heavy use.

Are your units genuine and under warranty?

Yes. All our products are genuine and come with the authorised dealer warranty. Contact us for warranty details per model.
